- **The leakage machinery is correct — do not touch it.** `forward_chaining_folds` produces
  day-disjoint, temporally-ordered folds even on unsorted input (I verified); `make_scaler()` is
  correctly a fresh **unfit** scaler to be fit inside CV on train folds only; `post_bg`/`elapsed_min`
  are correctly absent from `FEATURE_NAMES`; `pre_bg` is correctly continuous (no bin column). Do not
  "optimise" the scaler by fitting it once on the full set, and do not add a `pre_bg` state/bin input.
- **The IOB curve is correct — do not reshape it.** All 6 golden values match to 4 dp, monotone on
  (0,240), bounded [0,1]. The golden anchors and the monotonicity test are the guard against a silent
  curve change; keep them.
- **The exercise one-hot encoding is correct — do not collapse it to a numeric 0/1/2 column.** The
  "not scalar multiples" test is load-bearing; a numeric encoding mathematically forbids intense
  exercise raising BG.
- **`iob_at_start` derivation is correct.** It is computed from `boluses_before` (strictly before the
  event), excludes the correction's own bolus, and the clean-ISF filter still requires
  `food_in_window == False AND iob_at_start is not None AND < 0.5`. Do not hand-enter IOB, and do not
  drop the `food_in_window`/NULL exclusions.
- **INV-8's function in `core/safety.py` is meant to exist unused for now.** `inv8_beta_insulin_non_negative`
  was added at S-104; there is no fitting code to call it yet, and that is correct. Do not wire a model
  early to "use" it.
- **The binner tripwire skipping is correct** — it arms when `models/` lands (S-501/S-601). `features/`
  correctly does not trip it (no binner there). Do not force a binner name into `_BINNER_NAMES` yet.
- **N1's `# REPORTED`↔`_CLINICAL_TS` meta-guard is correct and must stay** — it is the forcing
  function that keeps the `now()` guard armed for every future reported timestamp. Do not remove it.
