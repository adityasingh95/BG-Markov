  - `305f967` feat(S-603) GREEN (Dev) — `tests/unit/test_bayesian_ordinal.py`: added `test_insulin_col_out_of_range_raises` and `test_too_few_states_raises`.

Left unrecorded, the git history reads cleaner than the process actually was.
