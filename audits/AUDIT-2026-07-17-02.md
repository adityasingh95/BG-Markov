Not through EPIC 7 as built, and this epic is precisely where that risk is adjudicated. I verified the load-bearing properties by **running them myself**, not by reading passing tests:

- **Leakage is guarded.** Driving the **actual** `fit_ordinal`→`predict_proba` through `temporal_cv`, every fold re-asserts `max(train)<min(test)` and day-disjointness; injected shared-day / out-of-order / shuffled folds raise `TemporalLeakageError`; the scaler is fit on train rows only.
