## ★ Section 1 — The INV-1 / Gate-2 retirement (S-1011, DL-035): full adjudication → LEGITIMATE

**What was done.** `inv1_prescriptive_requires_gate2`, `Gate2Status`, `gate2_status`,
`require_gate2`, and the gate call in `prescribe/bolus.py` were **removed** (not left dormant —
`tests/safety/test_bolus.py::test_inv1_is_gone_from_the_dose_path` asserts the symbol is
absent and the source contains no `require_gate2`/`gate2_status`). `core/safety.py:46-51` is a
comment marking INV-1 retired and stating the number is not reused. `recommend_bolus` now
raises a plain **`ValueError`** on a missing/`<= 0` ICR (`prescribe/bolus.py:67-71`).

**1. Authority — human-authorised, not agent-decided.** The charter lists "a gate needs
relaxing" as escalate-only. Evidence it was escalated and a human decided:
- DL-035: *"Requested by: operator … Status: ✅ EXECUTED 2026-07-18 … Execution (S-1011) was
  gated on an explicit operator 'go', which was given the same day."*
- DL-035 execution note: *"Operator go given; S-1011 executed the same day."*
- `docs/stories/S-1011.md:8-11`: *"at the operator's explicit and repeated direction."*
The agents surfaced the trade, wrote the safety argument, and waited for the operator's go.
This is the escalation discipline working, not a self-approved bypass.

**2. Clinical basis — the gate is moot, not bypassed.** Gate 2 existed to block dosing until a
**clinician-confirmed ICR** existed. That condition was satisfied *before* removal: DL-032
records ICR = 9 g/U and ISF = 30 confirmed 2026-07-17 by "operator relaying the
endocrinologist's sign-off" (explicitly *not* the 500/60≈8.3 heuristic). The operator's
standing direction to own clinical constants thereafter (DL-042: *"don't depend on an
endocrinologist for anything"*) is documented. So the ceremony was retired *after* the thing it
waited for had happened — the gate is moot, not circumvented.

**3. Residual safeguard — the dose is still bounded; one hole remains, by design.** With Gate 2
gone, what stands between a bad ICR and a bad dose (all independently executed, see Section 2):
- `ValueError` on `icr None/0/negative`, raised **before any arithmetic** → no div-by-null/inf.
- **INV-3** caps at 15 U and **flags** `implausible_input=True` (a carbs=900 typo → 15 U +
  flag, never a silent confident dose); never negative (→ 0.0).
- **INV-4** refuses any dose below BG 80.
- **S-1010** profile screen flags an out-of-expected-range ICR (7–10) and shows the previous
  value beside the new one (DL-048); every change is appended/audited (REQ-054).
- The full division is **shown**, framed as *"a suggestion for review"* to a person who has done
  this maths by hand for 30 years.
- **The one uncovered path:** a *plausible-but-wrong positive ICR* whose resulting dose stays
  under 15 U trips none of these and under/over-doses silently. DL-035 and S-1011's written
  argument both state this plainly and record it as an operator-accepted trade. **This is the
  safety floor that was deliberately lowered.** It is carried, not hidden (Residual Risk R1).

**4. Consistency — swept across the governing docs.** Verified the retirement is reflected, not
left stale, in the *authoritative* places:
- `CLAUDE.md:59,63,82` — INV-1 struck through, marked RETIRED, "number not reused."
- `README.md:83,100` — retirement noted.
- `docs/07-clinical-model-spec.md:39,262` — INV-1 marked RETIRED; §6 correctly notes this makes
  correction-derived ISF *more* important.
- `docs/03-state-model.md:136` — "Gate 2 — REMOVED."
- `docs/traceability.md:85` — the invariant table row marks INV-1 ⛔ RETIRED with the discrimination
  detail; `traceability.md:13` notes "INV-2..9 — INV-1 retired S-1011."
Stale narration survives only in **point-in-time historical story docs / trace test-columns**
(F1, F2) — a doc-hygiene issue, not a governing-spec inconsistency.

**5. TDD & ownership honesty.** Sequence is clean:
`de43818 docs(S-1011): story + safety argument (BA)` → `bb07031 test(S-1011): RED (SDET, tests/
