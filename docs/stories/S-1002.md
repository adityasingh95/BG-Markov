# S-1002 [SAFETY] — Patient readout UI

**Epic:** 10 · **Safety story:** Yes — **the first patient-reachable model surface** ·
**Status:** ✅ Done (2026-07-18) · **Requirements:** REQ-040, INV-2 · **Records:** DL-045 (the
pre-Gate-1 reading) · **Discharges the S-804 deferred presentation note** ·
**Rollback baseline:** `7a7c3bf`

> `prescribe/readout.py` has returned a tested `PatientReadout` since S-804 and nothing
> rendered it. This is the screen **she** looks at, possibly while low.

## Story
She opens a meal she has logged and sees, in plain words, whether a low is more likely than
usual — or an honest statement that there is nothing to tell her yet.

## ⚠️ A documented tension, resolved conservatively — escalated, not decided quietly

Two documents point different ways about what this page shows **before Gate 1**:

| Source | Says |
|---|---|
| `03 §3` (state machine) | CLOSED: *"Logging only. **No model. No output.**"* · Gate 0: *"Metrics computed. OPERATOR-VISIBLE ONLY. **Patient sees nothing.**"* |
| `10-backlog.md` S-1002 AC | *"before Gate 1 it renders the refusal / **baseline state** as a rendered answer, never a blank, never an error page"* |

Read one way, the AC asks for a **baseline BG projection shown to her today**. That would be
a new patient-facing clinical number, pre-Gate-1, never validated for her, on the screen she
is most likely to trust.

**This story takes the conservative reading**, and says so rather than picking silently:

- Before Gate 1 the page renders an honest **"nothing to tell you yet — keep logging"**
  state. That *is* "a rendered answer, never a blank, never an error page" — which is what
  the AC is actually guarding against — while honouring `03 §3`'s *"no output"*.
- **`BASELINE_FALLBACK` stays what S-804 built it for: the kill switch.** That is a
  different situation — she *has* been seeing output, it has been withdrawn, and the
  baseline is what remains. Reusing it pre-Gate-1 would put the same number in front of her
  in a state where she has never been told anything at all.

**Flagged for the operator.** If the intent really is to show her a baseline projection
during shadow mode, that is a change of scope on the patient surface and belongs in a
decision, not in a story's interpretation of an AC. Recorded as **DL-045**.

## Design

### `GET /meals/{meal_id}/readout` + `api/templates/readout.html`

The route has **two branches and no third**:

1. **Gate 1 closed** → render the "nothing yet" state. `build_patient_readout` is **not
   called at all** (it would raise, correctly). No model is loaded, no prediction is made,
   nothing is served. The page explains, without scolding, that the system is still
   learning and she should test as usual.
2. **Gate 1 open** → `serve_meal_prediction` (S-1008, INV-9: persisted before returned) →
   `build_patient_readout`, whose **first line is `require_gate1`**. The gate is therefore
   checked twice on this path, at the route and at the builder. That is deliberate
   belt-and-braces, not redundancy to be tidied away: the builder's check is the one that
   cannot be forgotten by a future second caller.

- **Hypo risk is the headline, in text.** `hypo_risk` and `severity` are already text
  labels (S-804); the template must not reduce them to a colour swatch.
- **No dose field exists on this screen. Ever.** `PatientReadout` has no `dose`/`bolus`/
  `units` attribute by construction (S-804); the template must not reintroduce the concept
  in prose either.
- Refusal and conflict render as **states with words**, per `05b §5.3`/`§5.4` — never a
  blank, and the system never picks a winner in a conflict.
- **Never scold** (`05b §8`). Not for a late reading, not for a missed meal.

## Written safety argument [SAFETY]

**Why the route branches instead of catching `GateNotPassed`.** Catching the exception and
rendering something would put the *rendering* decision downstream of a safety exception —
and the natural next refactor is to catch it somewhere broader and render *something
useful*. Branching first means the closed-gate path never constructs a readout at all, so
there is no object to accidentally render.

**Why the gate is checked twice.** The route's check decides which page to build; the
builder's check (S-804, its first line) is the invariant. Removing either leaves the other,
and only removing **both** could leak model output. A future caller that forgets the route
still hits the builder.

**Why no baseline number pre-Gate-1.** See the tension above. The failure this project
exists to prevent is a number that gets trusted and is quietly wrong. A pre-Gate-1 baseline
projection is a number she would have every reason to trust — the app showed it to her —
and no evidence has been gathered about how it performs for her yet.

**Why "no dose" is asserted on the template, not just the dataclass.** S-804 made a dose
structurally impossible to *pass*. Nothing stops a template from writing the word "units"
into a sentence. The check has to be where the text is.

## TDD strategy (SDET first — written and seen to fail)
`tests/safety/test_readout_ui.py`:
- **★ Gate 1 closed ⇒ 200 and the "nothing yet" state**, with **no** predicted state, no
  risk claim, and no baseline number. Never a 500, never a blank.
- **★ No bypass:** `?force=1`, `?gate1=open`, an `X-Force-Readout` header, and
  `BGMARKOV_FORCE_READOUT` / `SKIP_GATE` / `GATE1_PASSED` env vars all leave it closed.
- **★ `build_patient_readout` is never called while the gate is closed** — asserted by
  monkeypatching it to explode. The closed path must not construct a readout at all.
- **★ No dose-like token anywhere in the template source** (`dose`, `bolus`, `units`,
  `inject`) — the word, not just the field.
- Gate open ⇒ hypo risk is the headline, rendered as **text**.
- Refusal renders as a state with words (`05b §5.3`), not a blank.
- Conflict shows **both** and picks no winner (`05b §5.4`).
- Kill switch tripped ⇒ the baseline-fallback wording, and still no dose.
- **★ Nothing on the page scolds** — no "you should have", "you failed", "late", "missed".

`tests/a11y/test_readout.py`:
- `axe` clean; no horizontal overflow at 200% zoom; **risk is not signalled by colour
  alone** — she may be reading this while low, on a phone, in bad light.

**RED evidence:** `GET /meals/{id}/readout` 404s.

**Adversarial angle.** Two. (a) Catching `GateNotPassed` in the route and rendering "the
best we have" — helpful-looking, and it turns an invariant into a formatting concern.
(b) Reintroducing a dose in *prose* ("about 4 units would cover this") on a screen whose
dataclass was carefully built to make one impossible.

## Outcome (BA, 2026-07-18)

**Done.** `GET /meals/{meal_id}/readout` + `api/templates/readout.html`.
Commits: `33a6ed2` (BA + DL-045) → `780b81f` (SDET RED) → `274dae9` (Dev GREEN), SDET fixes
in between. `ruff` and `mypy --strict` clean.

**What she sees today.** *"Nothing to tell you yet. The system is still learning from what
you log. It will stay quiet until it has earned the right to say something useful. Test as
usual."* No number, no risk claim, no projection — the DL-045 reading, rendered.

**Three SDET defects, all of the same family: tests that were green for the wrong reason.**
1. **Seven of fourteen passed before the route existed.** Every *"X must not appear"*
   assertion was being made against a 404 body, where absence is free. These were the
   must-not-appear assertions — the ones most likely to be trusted later. Every one now
   asserts status 200 first.
2. **The post-Gate-1 tests patched the readout but not the gate**, so they exercised the
   *closed* branch and asserted against the wrong page. Fixed not by patching the gate but
   by opening it for real — 200 meals, 120 shadow days, a scored model, and a promotion
   through `POST /api/operator/promote` — so those tests now also prove the readout appears
   **only after a genuine promotion**.
3. **The a11y suite pointed at `/meals/1/readout`**, which does not exist on the live
   server, so axe was grading FastAPI's 404 JSON page. Five violations, none about this
   screen. The fixture now creates a meal through the API.

**★ The dose guard bit its own author, twice.** The template comment explaining that no
treatment amount may appear used the forbidden words itself — first *"dose"*, then *"how
much insulin"*. The fix was **not** to narrow the guard to rendered output. The comment was
rewritten to name none of them. A guard that is inconvenient exactly when you are writing
about it is a guard that works; a rule you have to reason about is a rule someone will
reason their way past.

**One thing the tests caught in the copy.** The template said *"Check your sugar as
usual"*; `05b §5.3`/`§5.4` say **"Test as usual."** The spec's wording won. Paraphrasing
patient-facing copy is how a "never scold / never suggest testing less" rule erodes a
sentence at a time.

**Guards seen to fire, then reverted.**
- The route catching `GateNotPassed` and rendering the best available thing ⇒ 4 fail.
- *"About 4 units would cover this"* added to the prose ⇒ 2 fail.
- A baseline projection shown pre-Gate-1 — the DL-045 reading reversed ⇒ 1 fails.
- *"You forgot to log yesterday"* ⇒ 1 fails.

**Deviations from the design above:** none.

**Open with the operator:** DL-045. If she *should* see a baseline estimate during shadow
mode, that is a scope change on the patient surface and wants a recorded decision, not an
AC re-reading.
