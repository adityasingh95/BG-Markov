# S-1019 — The idempotency key survives a double tap

**Epic:** 11 · **Safety story:** No (S-1016 carried the safety argument) · **Status:** ✅ Done (2026-07-30) ·
**Requirements:** REQ-001, REQ-002, REQ-006, REQ-013 · **Records:** DL-058 ·
**Found by:** `scripts/demo_ui.py` — two taps on *Log meal*, two meals, two boluses

> S-1016 made the server refuse a duplicate submission. It is correct, it is tested nine
> ways, and **it has never once been reached from the UI**, because `app.js` mints a fresh
> `crypto.randomUUID()` inside `payload()` — which runs once per submit.
>
> Two taps ⇒ two keys ⇒ two meals ⇒ two boluses.

## Story
A retried submission — a double tap, a nervous second press, a flaky connection — records
one meal and one bolus, from the browser, the way it already does from the API.

---

## Why the server-side fix was not enough

S-1016's key is the **client's** statement that *"this is the same submission as before"*.
A key generated at submit time cannot make that statement: it is a statement that every
submission is new. The server had no way to tell, and did exactly what it was told.

★ **The generalisable shape:** a guard whose input is supplied by the caller is only as
good as the caller. S-1016's tests all supplied the key themselves, so they tested the
server's half of a two-party protocol and none of the other half.

**Why it matters** (unchanged from S-1016, and now actually reachable): a duplicated bolus
corrupts `bolus_log`, `bolus_log` is the sole source of IOB, and IOB is **subtracted** from
the recommended dose. The demo measured it: IOB 7.26 → 14.26 U, dose 6.91 U → 0.00 U.
A double tap becomes an under-dose, hours later, with no error at any point.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The key is minted **once per form fill** and reused by every submit of that fill.
2. It is regenerated only after a **confirmed** save, so the next meal is a new submission.
3. It survives a reload mid-entry, alongside the draft — a restored draft that is
   resubmitted is the *same* submission.
4. Two taps on *Log meal* ⇒ one `meal_event` and one `bolus_log` row, **from a browser**.
5. The submit button is disabled while a request is in flight — belt as well as braces, and
   the only part of this she can see.
6. `POST /api/correction-events` takes an idempotency key and enforces it, because it also
   writes to `bolus_log`.

**Not in scope, stated rather than skipped:** `PATCH …/post-bg` is an update to a named
meal and is naturally idempotent. `POST /api/basal` treats a repeat date as a correction by
design (S-1013). `POST /api/operator/profile` appends versions and is operator-driven; a
duplicate version is untidy, not dangerous. None of the three writes a bolus.

## TDD strategy (SDET first)

`tests/e2e/test_double_tap.py`
- **★ Two `click()`s on the submit button ⇒ one meal AND one bolus row.** The assertion is
  on `bolus_log`, following S-1016: an implementation that dedupes the meal and re-inserts
  the boluses passes a meal-count test and preserves the entire harm.
- **★ Two separate, complete entries ⇒ two meals.** The failure mode of an over-eager fix,
  and the worse one: a key that never rotates silently discards her second breakfast.
- A reload mid-entry, then submit twice ⇒ still one meal.
- The submit button is disabled during flight and re-enabled after.

`tests/integration/test_correction_idempotency.py`
- A repeated correction key returns the first event and writes one `bolus_log` row.

`tests/unit/test_idempotency_key_js.py`
- **★ A grep guard: `randomUUID` does not appear inside the payload builder.** The exact
  line, in the exact place, that caused this.

**RED evidence:** the double-tap e2e test records 2 meals and 2 boluses.

**Adversarial angle.** (a) **Keying on the form contents** (a hash) instead of on a minted
id — then two genuinely identical meals an hour apart silently become one, and the second
is the one that goes missing. (b) **Disabling the button only**, which fixes the double tap
and not the retry. (c) Rotating the key on *any* response rather than a confirmed save, so a
failed submit cannot be safely retried.

## Outcome (BA, 2026-07-30)

**Done.** `api/static/submission-key.js`, the draft-boundary rotation rule in `app.js` and
`corrections.js`, `correction_event.idempotency_key` (UNIQUE, nullable, migration
`c7e1a09b4d22`), and the route's lookup + race branch + shared response builder.
Records **DL-058**.

### ★ The rotation rule took three attempts, and the two wrong ones failed in opposite directions
- **Rotate when the save returns.** A second tap landing just after a fast response carries a
  fresh key and logs a second meal. Caught intermittently by the e2e test.
- **Rotate on the next edit, tracked in memory.** The flag does not survive a reload, so a
  reload between two genuine meals reused the key and **the second meal vanished**, with a ✓
  on screen. The worse failure of the two — a duplicate is visible in the data, an absence
  is not.
- **The draft is the fill boundary.** Durable, and cleared only by a confirmed save.

### Guards seen to fire, on a clean committed tree

| Plant | Caught by |
|---|---|
| the key minted per submit again | the grep guard, **and** (after S-1021's rewrite) both dropped-response tests |
| dedupe the event but re-insert the bolus | `test_a_repeated_key_creates_one_event_and_one_bolus` — the assertion is on `bolus_log` |
| never rotate the key | `test_two_separate_entries_still_create_two_meals` |

The first plant is recorded in S-1021: on the tests as originally written it was **not**
caught by the browser at all.
