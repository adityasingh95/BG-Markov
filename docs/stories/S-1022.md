# S-1022 — The screens look designed, and selection state is visible

**Epic:** 11 · **Safety story:** No (but it is `05b §2` conformance) · **Status:** ✅ Done (2026-07-30) ·
**Requirements:** REQ-001, REQ-040, `05b §2`, `05b §5`, `05b §8` · **Records:** DL-060 ·
**Found by:** looking at the S-1018 screenshots

> Every favourite chip on the meal form shows a **✓**, all the time, whether it is
> selected or not.
>
> `aria-pressed` is set correctly by `app.js`. **No CSS reads it.** So the one thing the
> form has to tell her — *which dish did I just tap?* — is told to a screen reader and to
> nobody else.

## Story
The screens carry the design `05b` describes: state she can see, hierarchy she can scan,
and one visual language across patient and operator surfaces.

---

## Provenance

**The design source is the published artifact "BG-Markov — UI/UX prototype" (2026-07-18)** —
produced in this session, shown to the operator, and held **outside** the repository. Inside
the repo there is no prototype file: 259 commits, every branch, and only two passing mentions
(`10-backlog.md`, DL-034).

★ **This story was opened with the wrong claim.** It said *"there is no prototype"*, which is
true of the repository and false about the project, and it was reported as the second. The
operator corrected it and the artifact was recovered. *"I could not find it"* and *"it does
not exist"* are different statements, and a build that acts on the second when only the first
is established builds the wrong object confidently. Recorded here rather than quietly fixed,
because that is the failure worth remembering.

`docs/05b-ui-ux-spec.md` remains the **binding** source for accessibility. Where the two
disagree, `05b` wins and the divergence is written down at the point of divergence (DL-060).

---

## What is actually wrong

1. **★ Selection is invisible.** `.chip.favourite` renders `<span class="chk">✓ </span>`
   unconditionally and nothing hides it. Combined with Pico's default filled buttons, **all
   three favourites look identically chosen at all times.** The same applies to the timing
   presets and the before/after-eating pair.

   This is not cosmetic. `05b §2` puts her at BG 55 on a phone. The form's entire adherence
   argument (REQ-001: a repeat meal in ≤4 taps) rests on her being able to see that the tap
   registered. A control that looks the same before and after you press it is a control you
   press again.

2. **No hierarchy.** Every button is the same weight, so *Log meal* competes with *before
   eating* and with a favourite chip. On the operator screens, dense diagnostic content sits
   in undifferentiated prose.

3. **`05b §5`'s readout is a card with a headline and a distribution.** What renders is a
   left-bordered strip. The structure is right; the presentation carries none of the weight
   the spec's mockups give it.

4. **No dark mode.** `base.html` hard-codes `data-theme="light"` while `app.css` hard-codes
   light hex values. A phone in a dark room at 3am is exactly the case `05b §2` is written
   for.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An **unselected** chip shows no tick; the selected one does — and only one per group.
2. Selected and unselected differ by **more than colour** (`05b §2`): the tick, and a
   non-colour property (border weight) a colour-blind or greyscale reader can see.
3. One design language across patient and operator screens: a type scale, a card/surface
   treatment, one accent, and semantic tokens rather than scattered hex values.
4. **Every existing accessibility guarantee still holds**: axe clean on every screen, base
   font ≥ 18px, touch targets ≥ 48 × 48, no horizontal overflow at 390 px or 200 % zoom,
   and colour never the sole signal.
5. Light **and** dark, both AA.
6. **No template gains or loses meaning.** This story moves no clinical content and adds no
   model output — the INV-2 surface area is unchanged.

## TDD strategy (SDET first — written and seen to fail)

`tests/a11y/test_visual_state.py` — browser-driven, **computed styles**, because asserting on
a stylesheet's source text proves only that a rule was typed.

- **★ An unselected favourite renders no tick, and the selected one does.** RED: all three
  show one.
- **★ Pressed and unpressed differ in a NON-COLOUR property.** A test that only compares
  background colours passes on a design that is invisible in greyscale — which is the exact
  failure `05b §2` names.
- Only one chip per group is pressed, after two taps in the same group.
- The same for the timing presets and the before/after pair.
- **The primary action is visually dominant** — `Log meal` is wider than a chip, so the
  hierarchy is real and not just intended.
- The existing axe / font-size / touch-target / overflow tests must stay green on every
  screen, at both themes.

**Adversarial angle.** (a) **Signalling selection with colour alone** — the single most
natural way to do it, and a direct `05b §2` breach. (b) Restyling by shrinking: dropping a
button below 48 px or the base font below 18px to make the page look tidier. (c) Adding a
dark theme that only sets `background` and leaves the hard-coded `#1a1a1a` text unreadable.
(d) Asserting on `app.css` source text rather than on computed style.

## Outcome (BA, 2026-07-30)

**Done.** `api/static/app.css` rewritten as the prototype's design system; `base.html` drops
the hard-coded `data-theme`. Records **DL-060**.

### ★ Provenance, corrected
The story was opened saying *"there is no prototype"* — true of the repository, and wrong as
a statement about the project. **The operator had one: a published artifact,
"BG-Markov — UI/UX prototype" (2026-07-18), which this session produced and showed them.**
It was found in the session scratchpad and used as the source. The correction matters more
than the inconvenience: *"I could not find it"* is not the same claim as *"it does not
exist"*, and the first was reported as the second.

The tokens are now lifted from it directly — teal accent, paper/card/raised surfaces, 14px
radii, small-caps eyebrows, pill chips, brand dot, the mono working block, pill verdicts.

### Two deliberate divergences, both recorded in the stylesheet header
1. **48px touch targets**, not the prototype's 46 — `05b §2` is the binding number.
2. **A chosen chip carries a tick and a heavier border**, not the accent fill alone. The
   prototype signalled chosen state with colour only; that is invisible in greyscale.

> A prototype is a proposal. Where it disagrees with the accessibility spec, the spec wins.

### Guards seen to fire

| Plant | Caught by |
|---|---|
| chosen state signalled by colour alone (the prototype's own rule) | `test_pressed_and_unpressed_differ_by_more_than_colour`, and the before/after pair test |
| the tick hidden again | both tick tests |

### ★ Six layout defects found by running it, not by reading it
Four were the same shape — **a hard floor in `rem` that is fine on a laptop and takes the
page sideways on her phone at 200% zoom**: `minmax(14rem, 1fr)` on the metric grid (93px),
`min-width: 15rem` on the datetime field (232px), chips at `flex: 0 0 auto` (77px, because
"⭐ Dal + 2 roti + sabzi" is 385px wide at 200%), and `.gate-checklist li` at the flex
default `min-width: auto` (93px).

Two more came from looking at the screenshots: **the reported mealtime was clipped to
"0/2026, 11:20 AM"** by a `max-width` meant for a dose box — the one field ADR-8 exists to
protect, rendered unreadable — and Pico's `[role="group"]` stretched every chip into a slab,
erasing the size difference between *a choice* and *the action*.

### Still open
- `05b §5.1`'s mockup shows a **distribution** (Low / In range / High, with bars and
  percentages) on the readout. The readout renders a headline and a hypo-risk band, no bars.
  Adding them changes what `PatientReadout` carries, which is model-output shaping under
  INV-2 — a story, not a stylesheet change.
- The prototype's tabbed shell (Operator / Patient / Bolus) is not adopted; the real app
  uses routes, which is correct for a server-rendered app with no build step.
