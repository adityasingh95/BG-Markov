# S-1003 [SAFETY] — Bolus calculator UI

**Epic:** 10 · **Safety story:** Yes — **the most dangerous screen in the system** ·
**Status:** In progress · **Requirements:** REQ-042, REQ-043, INV-3, INV-4, REQ-020 ·
**Records:** DL-035 (Gate 2 retired) · **Rollback baseline:** `7a7c3bf`

> `recommend_bolus` has been tested since S-901 and nothing rendered it. This is the only
> screen that suggests putting insulin into a person who cannot feel a low.

## Story
She (or the operator) enters carbs and a current reading, and sees a suggested dose with
its full arithmetic — framed as something to check, never as an instruction.

## Design

### Structure: `api/bolus.py`, a separate router
**The dose path must import nothing from `models/`.** `api/app.py` imports
`models.metrics` and `models.shadow` for the operator dashboard, so putting the bolus
routes there would make *"no ML in the dose path"* untestable by inspection. A dedicated
router module makes it a **grep-able structural fact**: `api/bolus.py` imports only
`core`, `data` and `prescribe`.

### The four states, decided by branching — never by catching
1. **Profile incomplete** — no active profile, or `icr` null / `<= 0`. Renders *"set the
   ICR on the profile first"*. **Not a gate refusal** (Gate 2 is retired — S-1011/DL-035)
   and not a computing form. No dose.
2. **She is low** — `current_bg < BOLUS_BG_FLOOR` (80). Renders *"treat the low first"*.
3. **Implausible input** — the recommendation comes back `capped` + `implausible_input`
   (INV-3). Renders the **flag**, prominently, never a bare confident 15 U.
4. **A suggestion** — the dose, the full arithmetic, and the framing line.

**States 1 and 2 are checked *before* `recommend_bolus` is called**, so the route never
catches `ValueError` or `SafetyViolation`. This is the same discipline as S-1002, and it
keeps that story's source guard — *`api/app.py` catches no safety exception* — true of the
dose path as well. The invariants stay in `recommend_bolus` as the backstop for every
other caller.

### IOB is displayed, never typed — REQ-020 **[SAFETY]**
- Derived from `bolus_log` via `data.repositories.iob_at_start_at`, shown **read-only with
  its provenance** (which injections it came from).
- **No `<input>` for IOB exists in the template.** A typed IOB is a forbidden pattern
  (`detect_manual_iob`, S-105) because a remembered or guessed IOB is the most dangerous
  number on this screen: subtracting an IOB that is too high under-doses, and one that is
  too low **over-doses someone who cannot feel the result.**

### Reported time, not `now()` — ADR-8
The form asks **when she is going to inject**. IOB is computed at that reported time.
Defaulting to the system clock would be the forbidden pattern in its most tempting form:
it is *nearly* right when she uses the calculator at the table, and silently wrong every
time she uses it afterwards — which is exactly when IOB matters most.

### Nothing is logged, nothing is autofilled
The result is a rendered suggestion. There is **no button that carries the dose into a log
or an action**. A calculator that logs its own output turns a suggestion into a record of
what she took.

### `data/repositories.py::active_profile(session)`
The latest row by `effective_from` (REQ-054 — versioned, never overwritten). Returns
`None` when there is none, which is state 1.

## Written safety argument [SAFETY]

**Why branch instead of catch — again.** `recommend_bolus` raises `SafetyViolation` below
BG 80 and `ValueError` on a bad ICR. A route that caught them would be one refactor away
from rendering *"we couldn't calculate — here's an estimate"*. Branching first means the
refusing states never call the calculator, so there is no partially-computed dose in scope
to render. The invariants remain, unreachable from here but standing for every other caller.

**Why the implausibility flag must be loud.** INV-3 caps at 15 U. A cap alone would hand
back a confident, plausible-looking **15 U** for a `900 g` typo — a number that looks like
a decision rather than an error. The flag is the difference between the system saying *"15
units"* and *"this input looks wrong, please re-check"*. On this screen that distinction
is the whole safety margin.

**Why no IOB input.** It is the one field on this screen where a plausible wrong number
does direct harm. Everything else she enters is checkable against reality — carbs against
the plate, BG against the meter. An IOB she half-remembers is checkable against nothing,
and it is *subtracted*, so an underestimate silently increases the dose.

**Why no autofill into a log.** `bolus_log` is the source of truth for IOB (`04 §2`). A
dose written there because the calculator suggested it — rather than because she injected
it — corrupts every future IOB computation, and therefore every future suggestion, with no
error and no way to tell afterwards.

**What this does not do.** It does not use the model. It does not decide anything. It does
arithmetic she could do herself, more consistently than she can at 7am, and shows its
working so she can check it.

## TDD strategy (SDET first — written and seen to fail)
`tests/safety/test_bolus_ui.py`:
- **★ No profile / `icr` null / `icr = 0` / `icr < 0` ⇒ the profile-incomplete state**,
  with **no dose anywhere on the page** — and it must not read as a gate refusal
  (Gate 2 is retired).
- **★ BG 79 ⇒ "treat the low first" and no dose; BG 80 ⇒ computes.** The INV-4 boundary,
  exactly, on the screen.
- **★ `carbs_g = 900` ⇒ the flag is visible**, not a bare 15 U. Asserted on the rendered
  text: both the cap *and* the words that say the input looks wrong.
- **★ Five golden hand-computed doses render to 2 dp** — the arithmetic on screen is the
  arithmetic in the tests.
- **★ No IOB `<input>` exists in the template** (DOM-level, not just a grep) and IOB is
  shown with provenance.
- **★ `api/bolus.py` imports nothing from `models/`** (AST) — no ML in the dose path.
- **★ No form on the page posts the dose anywhere** — no autofill into a log or an action.
- The route never catches `SafetyViolation` / `ValueError` (source assertion, as S-1002).
- The framing line is present: *a suggestion for review, not an instruction*.

`tests/a11y/test_bolus.py`:
- `axe` clean; numeric inputs carry `inputmode="numeric"`; no horizontal overflow at 200%
  zoom; the implausible-input flag is **not** signalled by colour alone.

**RED evidence:** `GET /bolus` 404s; `api/bolus.py` does not exist.

**Adversarial angle.** Three. (a) An IOB input field, added because "sometimes you know it
better than the log" — the sentence that ends this system's safety story. (b) The cap
without the flag, because 15 U "is the safe answer anyway". (c) A *"log this dose"* button,
added for convenience, which quietly makes `bolus_log` a record of suggestions rather than
injections.
